Meeting Role Assignments

Assign roles to members. Selections add badges to their profile.

Select a meet, assign members to each role, then click Save Role Assignments. Saving will automatically advance the speech project of any assigned prepared speakers. Role badges are added to each member's profile. You can re-save to update assignments for the same event.

Voting Manager

Create anonymous post-meeting voting sessions and view results

After a meet, select the event and open a voting session — candidates are auto-filled from the role assignments. Members vote anonymously from their dashboards. Once voting closes, view results and use Award Badge to permanently add winner badges to member profiles.
