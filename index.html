About Us

We Turn Nerves Into Power

Ideas have within the power to change the world. However, even the most revolutionary of ideas take the skills of talented orators and leaders to turn them into reality. For instance, personal computing wouldn't be where it is now, without the persuasive genius and visionary leadership of Steve Jobs. Recognizing that, we here at Unscripted inculcate and nurture this very skill, that is: the exacting science as well as the art of public communication.


Anyone can attend our weekly meets as guest. So do join us to learn more about the various facets of this skill. For example, did you know that our words only account for 20% of the message we are trying to convey? The rest is delivered through tone and body language: We do practise all of that here at Unscripted. We maintain a fun, amicable and supportive environment rife with detailed positive feedback and pointers that keep us on track to be better communicators and great leaders.
